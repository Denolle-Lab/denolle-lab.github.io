## About Me
I am a Research Aide in the Quake Lab focusing on developing a method to detect, denoise, and characterize seismic events from FiberLab Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) data. I am extremely fascinated by DAS and its capability to enhance our understanding of Earth processes in both subsea and terrestrial settings.

## Research - Disciplinary Interests

- Marine Geophysics
- Machine Learning
